We're excited to introduce APEX (Approximate-but-Exhaustive search) [ https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24380 ], a novel search protocol designed to address the challenges of virtual screening in the era of ultra-large combinatorial synthesis libraries. In this post we’ll cover the motivation and underlying machinery behind APEX.
